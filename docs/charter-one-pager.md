# Charter Venue Partner: the two-minute version

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This summary explains the draft agreement in plain words. If anything here differs from the signed agreement, the signed agreement wins.

## What you get
Insert builds the **Venue Ops Stack** for [VENUE NAME] at **no build charge**:
1. A personal email reply to every wedding inquiry, with a tour link (we aim for under a minute; that is a target, not a promise).
2. Tour booking, reminders, and no-show follow-up.
3. Deposit requests and reminders that send **your own** payment link.
4. Review requests after the event.

You also get a direct line to the person who built it, and small changes (wording, timing, tour windows) are included.

**Email only to start.** Texting is not switched on at launch. It can be added later, once the carrier registration and your consent wording are approved. Any texting fees are passed through at our cost.

## What you pay
- **$300 per month, locked** for as long as you stay a partner.
- **Nothing until your stack is live.** Billing starts on the go-live date, not when you sign.
- **No percentage of your deposits.** We never touch your money. Couples pay you directly through your own payment link.
- Later customers pay $3,000-$4,500 to build and $500-$600/month. You skip the build fee and keep $300.
- A website refresh is a separate, optional project from $2,500. It is not part of this.

## What we ask
- Send us what we need to build (pricing rules, calendar access, FAQ answers, your payment link). We will give you a checklist.
- Approve every message template before it goes out. For the first 30 days every AI-drafted reply waits for your approval before it sends; after that you can switch to auto-send. AI-drafted messages say they come from your venue's assistant.
- Join up to a few short, honest feedback sessions in the first months.
- Once the stack is booking tours, consider giving a short testimonial in your own words. It does not have to be positive. We only use your name or logo with your written OK each time, and you can take it back.
- Make sure you have the right to message the people on your list (we help set up opt-in wording and STOP handling).

## Key dates
- **Go-live target: 45 days after we confirm we have everything we need from you.** If we miss it, you get free days for each day we are late, and if we are 30 days past the target you can walk away owing nothing.
- **Six-month minimum**, counted from go-live. After that, month to month.
- If we ever shut the service down, you can leave right away owing nothing.

## Your data
Your couples' and inquirers' information is **yours**. We use it only to run your stack, never sell it, and give you an export and delete it when you leave. We use outside services (hosting, email, text, AI) and keep them to the same rules.

## How to exit
- **Before go-live:** leave anytime, owing nothing.
- **After the six months:** 30 days' written notice, any reason.
- **During the first six months:** you can leave, but you owe the remaining months' fees, unless we have failed to deliver or fix a problem within 30 days of your notice.
- On exit you get your data export, and we delete the rest.
