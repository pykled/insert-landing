## 2. Scope of Services

2.1 **Included.** Insert will build, configure, test and operate for the Venue, at no build charge, the Venue Ops Stack:
1. **Inquiry auto-response**: a personalized reply to each new inquiry, with a tour link.
2. **Tour scheduling**: self-serve booking, reminders and no-show follow-up.
3. **Deposit requests and reminders**: messages that send the Venue's **own** payment link and remind couples to pay.
4. **Post-event review requests**: messages asking couples for reviews.

2.2 **Support and changes.** During the Charter Period Insert provides a direct line to the builder, fixes defects in the Stack, and makes reasonable configuration changes (template wording, tour windows, reminder timing, link changes) at no additional charge. "Reasonable" means changes that take about [2] hours or less per month in total; larger requests are quoted under 2.3.

2.3 **Not included (quoted separately).** Custom integrations (for example a CRM or booking system Insert has not already connected), new workflows or channels beyond the four above, bulk data migration, and anything needing new software development. Insert will quote these; neither party is obliged to proceed. **Website redesign/refresh** is a separate optional project, quoted on its own, from $2,500, under its own written agreement.

2.4 **No payment processing.** Insert does **not** process, receive, hold, or transmit funds, and does not take any percentage or fee on deposits or other payments. Couples pay the Venue directly through the Venue's own payment provider and link. The Venue is responsible for its payment provider account, contracts with couples, refunds, and chargebacks.

2.5 **Venue's responsibilities.** The Venue will deliver Content Intake promptly, answer reasonable questions within [5] business days, keep availability and pricing information current, keep its integrations connected, and review and approve templates.

2.6 **Charter selection.** Seats are by application. Insert may decline an application at its discretion before signing. Two charter seats are offered; this Agreement does not guarantee the Venue a seat beyond what is stated here.

## 3. Go-Live, Target Window, and Remedy

3.1 **Target.** Insert will use reasonable efforts to reach Go-Live within **forty-five (45) days after Content Intake Complete** (the "Target Date").

3.2 **Venue-caused delay.** The Target Date extends day for day for any period the Venue has not approved templates, not granted access, or not answered a request needed to proceed.

3.3 **Remedy if Insert misses the Target Date.** If Go-Live has not occurred by the Target Date for reasons other than Section 3.2 or Section 15 (force majeure):
(a) the billing start date (Section 4.2) moves back one day for each day of delay (no charge accrues during delay, which is already true because billing starts at Go-Live; the day-for-day credit is applied as a **free-month credit**, meaning the first [N] days of billing after Go-Live are waived, one day for each day late); and
(b) if Go-Live has not occurred within [30] days after the Target Date, the Venue may terminate this Agreement by written notice with no liability, no minimum term, and no payment owed.

## 5. Term, Minimum, and Termination

5.1 **Term.** The Agreement begins on the Effective Date. After Go-Live there is a **six-month minimum** (the "Minimum Term"), starting on the Billing Start Date. After the Minimum Term it continues **month to month**.

5.2 **Before Go-Live.** Either party may terminate before Go-Live by written notice with no payment owed, except the Venue returns or deletes Insert materials and Insert deletes Venue Data (Section 8.8).

5.3 **Early exit by the Venue during the Minimum Term.** The Venue may end the Agreement early. In that case the Venue owes the monthly fee for the remainder of the Minimum Term, **except** that no further fees are owed if the Venue terminates for Insert's uncured material breach (Section 5.5), for missed Go-Live (Section 3.3(b)), or under Section 5.6 (Insert's insolvency or discontinuance). *Drafter's note: a lawyer should confirm this early-termination amount is enforceable as a liquidated amount, not a penalty. A softer alternative is 50% of remaining fees.*

5.4 **Insert fails to deliver.** If Insert materially fails to deliver the Stack as described and does not fix it within 30 days of written notice, the Venue may terminate with no minimum, owes nothing further, and receives a refund of fees paid for any period in which the Stack was materially non-functional.

5.5 **Termination for breach.** Either party may terminate on written notice if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within 30 days of written notice (10 days for non-payment).

5.6 **Termination for convenience after the Minimum Term.** After the Minimum Term, either party may terminate for any reason on **30 days' written notice**. Insert will not terminate for convenience during the Minimum Term except on [60] days' notice with a refund of prepaid fees, and a Charter Venue Partner terminated by Insert without cause keeps access to its data per Section 8.8.

5.7 **Effect of termination.** Fees accrue through the effective date. Section 8.8 (export and deletion) applies. Sections that by nature survive (confidentiality, IP, liability limits, indemnity, governing law, 8.8) survive.

## 6. Messaging Compliance (SMS, Email)

6.1 **Who holds which obligation.** The Stack sends messages **in the Venue's name** to people who contacted the Venue. The Venue is the "sender" and decides who is messaged and why. The parties split the work as follows:

| Obligation | Venue | Insert |
|---|---|---|
| Having a lawful basis and consent for each recipient (an inquiry that gives an email address and, for SMS, a phone number with consent) | Responsible | Builds consent capture into forms/flows the Venue approves |
| Express written consent language for SMS where required | Approves wording | Provides draft wording and records the consent timestamp and source |
| Honoring STOP/opt-out | Responsible for accurate sender identity and not messaging opted-out people through other channels | Configures the Stack to honor STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE keywords and unsubscribe links, and to stop automated messages promptly |
| Sender identity (name, physical address, truthful headers) | Provides accurate name and address | Configures messages to display them |
| SMS carrier/10DLC-type registration | Authorizes registration in its name | Prepares the registration for the Venue to submit or submit as the Venue's agent |
| Template content | Reviews and approves every template before use | Drafts templates; will not send an unapproved template |

6.2 **Venue approval.** Insert will not switch on a message template until the Venue approves it in writing (email suffices). Material changes need new approval.

6.3 **Transactional vs. promotional.** Replies to inquiries and tour/deposit reminders are intended to be responses to a couple's own request. Review requests and any follow-up not requested by the couple may be treated as marketing under some laws; the Venue and Insert will configure those accordingly (consent where required, opt-out, identification).

6.4 **Laws.** Each party will comply with laws applicable to its role, including the federal Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227), the CAN-SPAM Act and FTC CAN-SPAM Rule, and California law. Insert gives no legal advice about messaging law; the Venue should consult its own counsel about its marketing practices.

6.5 **Response-time figures are targets.** The "40 to 60 seconds" reply time shown in marketing materials is a **design target** under normal conditions, not a guarantee or service level. Delivery depends on third parties (email/SMS carriers, the Venue's accounts, internet outages) and the couple's device. Insert makes no commitment on delivery, deliverability, response rate, or bookings.

## 7. AI-Generated Replies

7.1 **Disclosure.** The Stack uses AI tools to draft or personalize some replies. Insert will configure messages so that they do not state or imply a human wrote a specific message where that would be false, and will add a disclosure line if the Venue or applicable law requires one. [ATTORNEY TO ADVISE ON DISCLOSURE.]

7.2 **Guardrails.** Unless the Venue directs otherwise, AI replies are limited to: acknowledging the inquiry, confirming date availability from the Venue's calendar, sharing information in the Venue's approved FAQ, and offering the tour link. The Stack is configured **not** to: quote prices or discounts not in the approved pricing, make promises about contract terms, give legal/medical/safety advice, hold or release dates other than as the Venue configured, or reply to messages flagged as complaints or distress; those are routed to the Venue.

7.3 **Human-approval option.** The Venue may choose a mode where each AI-drafted reply waits for Venue approval before sending, and may switch modes on notice. Default mode: [AUTO-SEND within guardrails / HUMAN APPROVAL] for the first [30] days.

7.4 **Accuracy.** AI output can be wrong. Insert will test and monitor reasonably and will correct errors it learns of, but the Venue is responsible for keeping its calendar, pricing and FAQ accurate and for reviewing a sample of sent messages at least [monthly].

7.5 **No training on Venue Data.** Insert will not use Venue Data to train or fine-tune any AI model for use outside the Venue's own Stack, and will use providers whose terms do not permit them to train on Venue Data submitted through their business/API offering. ## 8. Data Protection

8.1 **Ownership.** As between the parties, the **Venue owns Venue Data**. Insert acquires no ownership of it.

8.2 **Roles.** For personal information of the Venue's couples, guests and inquirers, the Venue decides the purposes and means of processing and Insert acts as the Venue's **processor / "service provider"** under California law (including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended). The Venue is the "business" / controller.

8.3 **Limits on use.** Insert will process Venue Data only to provide the Stack to the Venue and as the Venue instructs in this Agreement or in writing. Insert will not: sell or share Venue Data (as those terms are defined in the CCPA); retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship between Insert and the Venue or for any commercial purpose other than providing the Stack; combine it with data from other sources except as the CCPA allows for a service provider; or use it to market Insert's or others' services to the Venue's couples. Insert may use **aggregated, de-identified** statistics about Stack performance (such as average reply time) that do not identify the Venue, couples or individuals.

8.4 **Consumer requests.** Insert will reasonably help the Venue respond to verified requests to access, delete, or correct data, and will pass along any request it receives directly within [5] business days.

8.5 **Subprocessors.** Insert uses third-party providers such as cloud hosting, email delivery, SMS delivery, scheduling/calendar, and AI model providers. Insert will keep a list of categories and, on request, names. Insert will bind each to written terms at least as protective as this Section 8 and remains responsible for their performance. Insert will give [15] days' notice of a new subprocessor; the Venue may object on reasonable data-protection grounds, and if not resolved, terminate without penalty.

8.6 **Security.** Insert will maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the data, including access controls, encryption in transit, and limiting access to people who need it. Insert does not promise any system is invulnerable.

8.7 **Incidents.** Insert will notify the Venue without undue delay, and in any event within [72] hours, after confirming a breach of security affecting Venue Data, with the information then known, and cooperate in notices the Venue must give.

8.8 **Export and deletion on exit.** Within [30] days after termination or on request, Insert will provide the Venue an export of Venue Data in a common format (CSV/JSON) at no charge, and then delete Venue Data from active systems within [30] days after export (backups within [90] days), and confirm deletion in writing, unless law requires retention.

8.9 **Sensitive data.** The Venue will not send Insert payment card numbers, government IDs, or health data. Payment details are handled only by the Venue's payment provider.

8.10 **Responsibility for notices.** The Venue is responsible for its own privacy notice to couples and for any consents it must obtain. Insert will supply a short description of the Stack's processing to help.
