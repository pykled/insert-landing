The Venue Ops Stack

From the first hello to a kind review.

Four automations, written in your voice and approved by you, that carry a couple from inquiry to review. They work with the tools you already use.

Shown below: Willow Creek Estate and its couple, an illustrative example.

  1. i.

    A reply, usually within the minute

    A couple writes at eleven on a Sunday night. We aim to have a personal answer with them in under a minute: their date, their guest count, and a link to choose a tour.

    Sample

    Willow Creek EstateSunday, 11:02 pm

    Dear Maya and Jordan, congratulations!

    October 17 is open, and 120 guests suits our garden ceremony and barn reception beautifully. Choose a time to visit: willowcreek.co/tour

    Re: October 17Typically under a minute

  2. ii.

    The tour, arranged

    They pick a time that suits them. Reminders follow, and a gentle reschedule note goes out if a visit is missed.

    Your visitConfirmed

    Thursday, June 11 at 4 pm

    Park by the barn; Elena will meet you at the garden gate.

    Reminders: the day before, two hours before

  3. iii.

    A deposit request, through your own link

    After the tour, a deposit request goes out while the evening is still fresh, with a reminder if it’s needed. Couples pay you directly through your own payment link; Insert never touches the money.

    Deposit requestOctober 17, 2027

    $2,000

    Payable to Willow Creek Estate through the venue’s own payment link.

    Reminder follows if neededPaid to you directly

  4. iv.

    A thank-you, and a kind word

    A week after the wedding, a warm note thanks them and asks for a few words on Google or The Knot.

    EmailSeven days after

    Hi Maya and Jordan, it’s Elena at Willow Creek. Still smiling about Saturday. A few words about your day would mean the world to us.

    Review link included

What stays yours

The room, the light, the story of the place.

You step in for the tour and the wedding day. Everything in between runs on its own, in words you approved before anything was sent.
