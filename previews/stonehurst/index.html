The property

A garden planted by hand, on the corner of Sunland and Stonehurst.

The name comes from the street and from the hand-laid stone work that dances across the main pathways.

The barn is one of the oldest structures in this part of Los Angeles County and hosted some of the area's original Boy Scout meetings in 1907; its doors were reclaimed from the original horse stalls. Most of the vegetation, from the towering South American fig over the flower garden to a cactus garden that fruits every spring and fall, was planted by hand by Joe Stromei, whose name is still carved into a beam of the greenhouse.

What is the maximum capacity?

We can host up to 200 guests.

Are there multiple events in one day?

No. The venue is private and we host only one event a day.

Can all six spaces be used?

For private events we allow three to four spaces to be used, unless otherwise approved.

What is the rental time for events?

Our standard is a 12-hour rental including breakdown. On event days the gates open at 12 PM, the event ends at 10 PM, and there are two hours for breakdown (10 PM to midnight). For additional hours or shorter time frames, send us a note.

Is a wedding or event planner required?

Yes. We require, at minimum, a partial-service planner for your event.

What is included in the rental?

Valet, a site manager, luxe restrooms and security are included in the rental.

How do I inquire about filming and photoshoots?

For filming and photoshoots, email us and we'll come back with availability and rates for your dates.
