## (a) Scorecard

Each criterion is scored 0-3 and multiplied by its weight. Maximum is 39.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 3 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Volume proxy | x3 | 100+ combined reviews, multiple weddings visible monthly, 40+ weddings/yr implied. Under ~25/yr = disqualify |
| 2 | Revenue per wedding | x2 | public price/package roughly $6k-$25k+. Under ~$3k = weak |
| 3 | Decision-maker access | x2 | independent, 1-5 people, owner/manager findable. Hotels, resorts, chains, city/foundation/board venues, caterer-exclusive = DQ |
| 4 | Response/booking gap | x2 | form buried or absent, call-for-pricing, no tour scheduler, Gmail/Yahoo email, no date check. Slick funnels score low |
| 5 | Quality/brand fit | x2 | strong photography, 4.7+ rating, case-study worthy |
| 6 | Learning value | x1 | one-event-per-day or multi-space, seasonality, mixed sizes |
| 7 | Geography | x1 | within ~75 min of SGV (3 = under ~60 min, 2 = 60-80, 1 = 80-95) |

Unverified evidence is scored conservatively (usually 0-1 on that criterion), never guessed up.
