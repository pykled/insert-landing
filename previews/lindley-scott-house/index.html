Our Story

Built for a ranch president. Kept for celebrations.

The Lindley-Scott House was built in 1911 for Charles Lindley, president of the Foothill Ranch. In 1924, Mabelle Scott Anderson, owner of the Mabelle Scott Rancho School for Girls, acquired the house.

Since 1978 the estate has hosted weddings and receptions for families across the San Gabriel Valley, from Azusa and Glendora to Pasadena, Arcadia, and Monrovia. We host only one event per day, so the whole property is yours.
